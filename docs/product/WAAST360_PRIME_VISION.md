# WAAST360 Prime Vision

## The Prime Architecture
WAAST360 Prime represents the full-featured, enterprise-grade iteration of the platform. Although Lite is the immediate focus, all structural decisions must support the eventual deployment of Prime features.

## Anticipated Prime Features
- Deep multi-tenancy (Organization -> Company -> Branch)
- Advanced AI validation algorithms.
- Full inventory and complex GST reconciliation.
- Multi-Tally Instance orchestration.
- Advanced role-based access control and approval workflows.
- Forecasting, enterprise analytics, and advanced reporting.
