# WAAST360 Lite Scope

## Delivery Goal
Deliver a working demonstration within 3–5 days.

## In Scope (The Golden Path)
- Tally connection
- Company discovery
- Invoice upload
- AI extraction
- Accounting proposal
- Validation
- Duplicate detection
- Human approval
- Tally posting
- Read-back verification
- Audit trail

## Out of Scope for Lite (Do Not Implement Yet)
- Complete inventory management
- Complete GST suite
- Complete AR/AP tracking
- Advanced procurement
- Forecasting & Budgeting
- Enterprise analytics
